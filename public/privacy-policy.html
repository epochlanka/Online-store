Privacy policy

Last updated:

This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website, how we use it, and the choices you have. Template text - have this reviewed before launch to match your business and the applicable Sri Lankan data protection law.

Information we collect

  • Order details: name, email, phone number, delivery and billing addresses, items purchased.
  • Account details: if you create an account, your login email and a securely hashed password.
  • Payment information: card payments are processed by our payment provider. We do not receive or store full card numbers or CVV codes.
  • Communications: messages you send us and your newsletter preferences.

How we use your information

  • To process, deliver and support your orders.
  • To send order confirmations and delivery updates.
  • To send marketing emails only if you have opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To prevent fraud and keep our website secure.

Sharing

We share only what is necessary with courier partners (to deliver your order) and payment providers (to process payment). We never sell your personal data.

Data retention & security

We keep order records as required for accounting and legal purposes. Data is stored on secure servers, transmitted over HTTPS, and access is limited to authorised staff.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you logged in and remember your cart. We do not use advertising cookies without your consent.
