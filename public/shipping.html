Delivery options

  • Standard delivery - delivered within the estimated working days shown above.
  • Express delivery - priority dispatch for an additional fee ().
  • Store pickup - collect free of charge from our store once we notify you that your order is ready.

Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, we'll email you the courier name and tracking number. You can also check progress on the Track order page.

Failed deliveries

If the courier cannot reach you after two attempts, the parcel is returned to us and we will contact you to arrange redelivery. An additional delivery fee may apply. Cash-on-delivery orders that are refused may restrict future COD orders.
