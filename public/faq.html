Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you need? Contact us.

Orders

How do I place an order?
Choose your size and colour on the product page, add it to your cart and proceed to checkout. You can check out as a guest or with an account.
Can I change or cancel my order?
Contact us as soon as possible. We can change or cancel orders that have not yet been dispatched.
How do I track my order?
Use the Track order page with your order number and email, or view it in your account. We also email you when your order is dispatched.

Delivery

Do you deliver islandwide?
Yes, we deliver to all 25 districts in Sri Lanka. Delivery times and fees depend on your district - see Delivery information.
Is there free delivery?
Orders above the free-delivery amount shown in the cart qualify for free standard delivery.
Do you ship internationally?
Not yet - international shipping is coming soon.

Payments

Which payment methods do you accept?
Cash on delivery, bank transfer, and Visa / Mastercard through a secure payment gateway.
Is it safe to pay by card?
Yes. Card payments are processed on the payment provider's secure page. We never see or store your card number or CVV.
How does bank transfer work?
After you place your order, we show our bank details. Use your order number as the reference and send us the slip. We dispatch once payment is confirmed.

Sizing & products

How do I find my size?
Each product page has a size guide with body measurements. If you are between sizes, we recommend sizing up for a relaxed fit.
How should I care for my clothes?
Care instructions are listed on each product page and on the garment label. Most items are best washed cold and line dried in shade.

Returns

Can I return or exchange an item?
Yes, within 14 days of delivery for unworn items with tags attached. See our Returns & exchanges policy.
