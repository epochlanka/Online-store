Returns & exchanges

We want you to love what you ordered. If something isn't right, you can exchange or return it within 14 days of delivery.

Conditions

  • Items must be unworn, unwashed and in original condition with all tags attached.
  • Underwear, swimwear, earrings and items marked "final sale" cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.
  • Proof of purchase (your order number) is required.

How to request a return or exchange

  1. Log in to your account and choose "Request return" on a delivered order, or contact us with your order number.
  2. We'll confirm your request and arrange a courier pickup or give you our return address.
  3. Once we receive and inspect the item, we'll send your exchange or process your refund.

Refunds

Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 working days to your original payment method. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded by bank transfer. Original delivery fees are non-refundable unless the item was faulty or incorrect.

Faulty or incorrect items

If you received a faulty or incorrect item, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with photos. We'll cover all return delivery costs.
